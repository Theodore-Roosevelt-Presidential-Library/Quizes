## Verification pipeline

Every quiz clears this before it ships. Standard adapted from
`/Users/mbriney/TRPL/otd-social/references/verification.md`.

1. **Two independent sources per dated claim** — one book in the 359-title corpus at
   `/Users/mbriney/TRPL/Book_Text/`, one external authority (Library of Congress,
   National Park Service, Theodore Roosevelt Center, Federal Judicial Center).
2. **Every TR quote verbatim or cut.** Period spelling preserved. Cite speech, letter,
   or book with date. If exact wording can't be established, describe the idea instead.
3. **Confidence rating per question** — HIGH / MEDIUM-HIGH / MEDIUM. Nothing below
   MEDIUM ships.
4. **Independent review pass** on the finished copy, with no prior context, assuming
   nothing has been checked.
5. **Distractor audit** — each of the three wrong options must be unambiguously wrong.
   The most common failure mode is a distractor that is arguably true.

Known traps already documented: the "banana" quote is not TR's; "an entire regiment of
cowboys" is a newspaper's words, not his; the boat thieves got twenty-five months, not
three years. Several famous Badlands photographs are posed re-creations.

## Format

One engine, one format — 15 questions, 4 options. Variety comes from how questions are
written (image-led, quote-led, myth-busting), not from new code. Every quiz is
embeddable, challengeable, and playable live from day one.
