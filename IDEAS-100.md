# A hundred more quizzes

The first sixteen mapped the `/tr` section. That axis is now spent — cutting the
biography thinner produces quizzes that compete with each other for the same pages
and get progressively less interesting.

These hundred move along four different axes instead: **occasion** (a reason to post
today), **audience** (who is playing), **difficulty** (a reason to come back), and
**the world around him** (subjects where TR is the way in, not the whole story).

Grouped by what they're for, not by topic.

## What I would not build

**More slices of the core biography.** Seventeen through twenty-five would each be
weaker than the sixteen we have and would compete with them for placement.

**Anything scored on harm.** Complex Legacy stays off the list, for the reason
already agreed: a badge saying you got 12/15 on the subject is the wrong artefact.

**Quizzes with no page to sit on.** Every one of these should have a home on
trlibrary.com. If there is no page, write the page first — the quiz is a reason to
visit it, not a substitute for it.

---

## The honest constraint

A hundred quizzes is **1,500 questions**, every one needing two-source verification.
At fifteen questions each that is not a content project, it is a production line, and
the Badlands audit showed what happens when the checking comes after the writing
rather than during it.

Three things would have to change:

1. **Variable length.** Not everything needs fifteen. The Daily is one question;
   True or False could be ten. Reserve fifteen for the flagship subjects.
2. **A verification pass that runs on the corpus automatically** — extract every
   quotation and figure, grep, flag the misses for a human. The audit script written
   for this project is the seed of that.
3. **Hagedorn's *Roosevelt in the Bad Lands* (1921) in the corpus.** It is the
   eyewitness source for the Dakota period and its absence already cost us four
   quotations.

## If you only did ten

Group B, the calendar. Twenty occasions, twenty guaranteed posting days, and each one
is a reason for someone to come back who has already played. That is worth more than
another twenty subject quizzes nobody has a reason to open on a Tuesday.
